A little update from your man

I'm a little
under the weather.

My love, I haven't been feeling my best physically today. I think I may have a fever, so I'm taking it easy and trying to rest.

Your smile deserves its own sunshine

Don't forget
to smile, okay?

Even if today is ordinary. Even if something didn't go as planned. Even if the world feels like it is moving too fast.

I hope something small makes you laugh today. I hope someone is kind to you. I hope you eat something delicious, feel beautiful, and remember how precious you are.

You deserve gentle days, my Queen.

For the days ahead

Better days
are coming.

Life won't always feel easy. We may have days when we're tired, days when things don't go according to plan, and days when we simply need to pause.

But we don't have to solve our whole future in one day.

We can take one step. Make one dua. Do one good thing. Rest. Then try again tomorrow, InshaAllah.

There is still so much goodness ahead of us.

A prayer for my Queen

Ya Allah…

Protect my Sarauniya and bless her with good health, happiness, peace of mind, and a heart that remains close to You.

Grant her success in all that is good. Keep sadness, harm, and hardship away from her as much as You can. Bless her family and grant her beautiful surprises from places she never expected.

And Ya Allah, grant me shifaa, restore my health, and allow us to see many beautiful days together.

Make our intentions sincere, our steps blessed, and our journey to Nikkah easy if it is خير for us.

Ameen, Ya Rabb. 🤲
